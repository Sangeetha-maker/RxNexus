# Drug Utilization & Formulary Optimization Intelligence

## CMS Drug Utilization PUF
The CMS Medicare Part D Prescriber Summary Public Use File (PUF) aggregates prescription claims across physicians and drug formulations.

## Core Financial & Volume Indicators
- **Aggregate Drug Spend**: Total expenditures across all Medicare Part D plans for a specific brand or generic medication.
- **Total Standardized 30-Day Claims**: Normalized fill volume allowing comparison between 30-day and 90-day supplies.
- **Average Cost Per Claim**: Unit economic metric calculated as:
  $$\text{Average Cost Per Claim} = \frac{\text{Total Drug Spend}}{\text{Total Claims}}$$

## Formulary Placement & Restriction Strategies
- **Prior Authorization (PA)**: Implemented on high-cost or specialty drugs to confirm FDA indication, clinical necessity, and prevent off-label overutilization.
- **Step Therapy (ST)**: Requires trial of a first-line preferred agent (e.g., Metformin for diabetes, generic statins for cholesterol) prior to approving higher-cost second-line agents (e.g., SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 agonists).
- **Quantity Limits (QL)**: Restricts monthly pill counts to prevent stockpiling and align with FDA-approved dosing schedules.
- **Alternative Review Candidates**: Lower-tier generic or biosimilar therapeutic alternatives that offer immediate plan savings while maintaining clinical safety.
