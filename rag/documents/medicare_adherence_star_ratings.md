# CMS Medicare Part D Star Ratings & Medication Adherence Framework

## Star Ratings Adherence Measures (CMS Part D)
CMS evaluates Medicare Advantage and Prescription Drug Plans on key clinical adherence triple-weighted measures:
1. **Medication Adherence for Diabetes Medications**: Proportion of Days Covered (PDC) >= 80% for non-insulin anti-diabetic agents.
2. **Medication Adherence for Hypertension (RAS Antagonists)**: PDC >= 80% for ACE inhibitors, ARBs, and direct renin inhibitors.
3. **Medication Adherence for Cholesterol (Statins)**: PDC >= 80% for statin therapy.
4. **Statin Use in Persons with Diabetes (SUPD)**: Ensuring diabetic members receive guideline-directed statin therapy.

## Measuring Gaps in Therapy & Refill Regularity
- **Refill Gap Days**: Elapsed days between when a prior fill's day supply expired and when the subsequent refill was dispensed.
- **Proportion of Days Covered (PDC)**: Number of days covered by prescription fills divided by total days in the observation window.
- **Impact on Payer Economics**: Poor adherence leads to preventable hospitalizations, exacerbations, emergency admissions, and lower CMS Quality Bonus Payments.
