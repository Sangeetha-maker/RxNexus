# Pharmacy Network Distribution & Dispensing Fee Intelligence

## Overview
Medicare Part D sponsors contract with retail and mail-order pharmacies to form provider networks under CMS guidelines.

## Pharmacy Network Architecture
- **In-Network Retail Pharmacies**: Standard retail pharmacy access meeting statutory convenience standards.
- **Preferred Retail Pharmacies**: Selected network pharmacies offering lower member cost-sharing (e.g., $0 copay for Tier 1 generics) in exchange for discounted dispensing fees.
- **Mail-Order Networks**: 90-day maintenance medication dispensing facilities designed to improve chronic disease adherence.

## Dispensing Fee Benchmarks
- Negotiated dispensing fees typically range from **$0.40 to $1.20** per 30-day generic or brand prescription.
- Regional Alliances:
  - **Northeast Regional Network**: High density urban retail access (e.g., CVS, Duane Reade).
  - **Southeast Retail Network**: Preferred community access (e.g., Walgreens, Publix).
  - **South Central Network**: Supermarket and high-volume retail chains (e.g., Walmart, HEB).
  - **West Coast Network**: Regional grocer and pharmacy networks (e.g., Safeway, Rite Aid).
  - **Midwest & Community Pharmacy Alliance**: Independent and community pharmacy networks.

## Payer Optimization Strategy
Payers analyze pharmacy network data to:
1. Increase the proportion of claims filled at **preferred retail pharmacies** to lower aggregate dispensing costs and member out-of-pocket friction.
2. Monitor geographic dispensing access to ensure network adequacy across urban, suburban, and rural ZIP codes.
